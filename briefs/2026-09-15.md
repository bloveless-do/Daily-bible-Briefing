## International

**1. Iran war grinds on, Yemen fighting endangers Red Sea shipping.** The US-Iran war is in its seventh month with the Strait of Hormuz still effectively blockaded and oil prices climbing. Iran is demanding war reparations and sanctions relief as preconditions to end its blockade; talks are stalled. Saudi Arabia struck Yemen on Tuesday; a Houthi official said the strikes killed three people, including two children, a claim AP could not independently verify. Human Rights Watch has accused the Houthis of possible war crimes over their own attacks on commercial vessels in the Red Sea. (AP, Reuters)

## National

**3. Fed expected to raise rates Wednesday after hot August inflation.** August CPI came in at 3.4% annual, core at 2.4%, with gas prices, up sharply amid the Iran war, a leading driver. A Reuters poll found 85% of economists expect a quarter-point hike to 3.75%-4.00% at Wednesday's meeting, the first increase since July 2023. (Reuters)

**4. Congress punts the government funding fight past the midterms.** Lawmakers passed a stopgap bill funding the government through December 11, averting an October 1 shutdown. The House has passed three of twelve FY2027 spending bills; the Senate has passed none. (Bloomberg, NPR)
