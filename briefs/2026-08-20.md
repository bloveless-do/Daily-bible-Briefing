## International

**1. Trump declares "economic warfare" on Iran, threatens any country that trades with it.** Trump called it the "most crushing economic operation ever taken against any country," saying any nation whose banks, businesses, airports, or government entities give Iran "any type of lifeline" will itself face "TREMENDOUS Economic Consequences." The US special envoy to Syria, Tom Barrack, called the strikes "an unnecessary escalation that does not advance regional stability." Qatar, Egypt, and Saudi Arabia condemned the strikes. (Reuters, NPR, AP)

## Local and California

**6. Chino Hills sales tax measure headed to November ballot.** The city council voted unanimously in July to place a one-cent Transactions and Use Tax on the Nov. 3 ballot, projected to raise $11.5 million a year. The city's 2026-27 general fund budget shows $59.2 million in revenue against $63.5 million in expenditures, a $4.3 million projected deficit. (Champion, unrated local)

**7. Chino's drone fireworks enforcement produced 18 citations at $1,000 each.** Police Chief Kevin Mensen told the council the department scaled up from a single test drone last year to multiple drone teams backed by 65 officers this Fourth of July. State law caps the fine at $1,000 for anyone holding under 25 pounds of illegal fireworks, which is why the citation total looks small next to the enforcement effort. (Champion, unrated local)
