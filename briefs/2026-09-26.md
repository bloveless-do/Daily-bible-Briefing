# Daily Brief — Saturday, September 26, 2026

Trump told the UN he may "annihilate" Iran if no deal comes; the war he promised to end in weeks is in its seventh month. ## International

**1. Trump threatens to "annihilate" Iran, war has no end date.** At the UN General Assembly Tuesday, Trump said he faces a choice: let Iran "rebuild and create a far greater country," or "annihilate the Islamic Republic, and do it quickly." The war began with US and Israeli strikes on February 28 and is now in its seventh month; Trump originally said it would be over in four or five weeks. His own UN ambassador said this week there is still no timeline for ending it. Iranian President Masoud Pezeshkian responded the next day that Iran will not bend to "intimidation" and called his country a victim of terrorism. World leaders in the chamber reacted mostly with silence. (Axios, Washington Post)

**2. US pushes to revive Russia-Ukraine ceasefire talks after deadly strikes.** Russian strikes killed several people in Kyiv and other Ukrainian cities this week as the aerial war intensified. Washington is trying to arrange a new round of trilateral talks between the US, Russia, and Ukraine, with an energy-infrastructure ceasefire and reopening the Black Sea grain corridor as first steps. Putin says all proposals remain "on the table" but Moscow is still weighing its interests. (AP, Axios)

## National

**3. CNN left off Air Force One days after winning back White House access.** A federal judge ruled Thursday that the administration likely violated the Fifth Amendment when it revoked hard passes for CNN, Politico, and MS NOW reporters, and ordered access restored for 14 days. The order covered building access, not pool assignments. On Friday the White House signaled CNN would not fly with Trump on Saturday's trip, the latest move in the standoff. (NPR, Axios)

**4. James Copenhaver, wounded in 2024 Trump rally shooting, dies at 76.** Copenhaver was hit in the arm and abdomen at the Butler, Pennsylvania rally where a gunman tried to assassinate then-candidate Trump. He needed multiple surgeries and had lasting kidney and leg damage. He died Wednesday at a Pittsburgh hospital. (NPR)
