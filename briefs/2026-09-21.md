# Daily Brief — Monday, September 21, 2026

Trump says he's deciding between a deal, economic strangulation, or wiping out Iran entirely, ahead of a UN week where he meets Gulf leaders Tuesday. Ukraine hit Moscow with its largest drone attack of the war. Chino Hills' 1-cent sales tax measure is officially on the November ballot.

## International

**1. Trump weighs "wiping out" Iran, heads into UN week isolated.** Trump cut short a weekend at Camp David and told Fox News he is in "deciding mode" on Iran, laying out three options: a deal, "letting them rot economically," or "when do I blow the entire nation up." The State Department issued a fresh warning that the security environment in the Middle East could face "unforeseen escalation." Trump meets Tuesday with leaders from Saudi Arabia, the UAE, Qatar, Bahrain, Kuwait and Oman on the sidelines of the UN General Assembly before deciding how to proceed. (The Hill)

**2. Ukraine's largest drone attack of the war hits Moscow.** Ukraine fired more than 1,000 drones at Russia overnight Saturday, hundreds aimed at Moscow, timed to the final day of Russia's parliamentary elections. Moscow's mayor called it the largest attack yet on the capital; the Moscow Oil Refinery and a residential building were damaged. Two people were killed and 20 wounded across the wider Moscow region. (NPR, The Hill/AP)

**3. Houthi missile fired at Riyadh, intercepted.** Saudi Arabia confirmed Yemen's Houthis attempted a ballistic missile attack on Riyadh, the first targeting of the Saudi capital since fighting escalated in July. Saudi air defenses intercepted it near the airport; no casualties or damage reported. The Houthis also targeted Yanbu, Taif, Bisha and Farasan; those were also intercepted. (NPR)

## National

**4. Trump signs Graham sanctions act targeting Russia and Iran.** Trump signed the Lindsey O. Graham Sanctioning Russia and Iran Act into law, passed 86-11 in the Senate and 262-159 in the House. It authorizes tariffs up to 100 percent on countries buying Russian oil or gas or helping Russia evade sanctions, hitting China and India hardest as the largest buyers. It targets Russia's "shadow fleet" of tankers and extends Iran sanctions five years. (The Hill)

**5. FAA rolls out AI air traffic system starting today.** The FAA begins deploying its SMART system in the Washington, D.C. area Monday, ahead of a nationwide rollout, part of an $875 million, 12-year contract with Air Space Intelligence. The software analyzes flight schedules, weather and runway capacity to predict congestion before it happens. It is built to work alongside controllers, not replace them. (NewsNation)

**6. Weight-loss supplement recalled over undeclared toxic ingredient.** Lipofit Extreme Fat Burner 2.0 is under nationwide recall after FDA testing found undeclared fluoxetine in the daytime tablets and DNP, a banned industrial chemical, in the nighttime tablets. DNP can cause fatal heart rhythm problems and cardiac arrest. Affected lot number is 25M12F, expiration September 2027. (The Hill)

## Local and California

**7. Chino Hills sales tax measure set for November ballot.** Measure J would raise the local sales tax 1 point, from 7.75 to 8.75 percent, generating an estimated $11.5 million a year for the city. Groceries, rent, medicine, labor and utilities are exempt. The city is projecting a $4.3 million structural budget shortfall for fiscal year 2026-27. The measure includes annual independent audits and public spending disclosure. (Champion, unrated local; Ballotpedia)

**8. Chino Council floats memorial park for slain officer.** Councilmember Curtis Burton proposed a Russell M. Miller Sr. Memorial Park at the Sept. 1 Chino City Council meeting, honoring the 12-year Chino PD veteran killed by a drunk driver in 2000. Mayor Eunice Ulloa suggested a police theme throughout, including child-size patrol cars. (Champion, unrated local)
