## National

**3. She cited the Supreme Court's June ruling striking down his broader 2025 order. The administration can still develop implementation guidance but cannot enforce the new policy for now. (Forbes)

**4. War costs mount for Trump ahead of midterms.** Reuters/Ipsos polling puts Trump's approval on the economy at an all-time low of 29%, down from the mid-30s in January and February. Democrats are campaigning on the price at the pump as proof Republicans haven't delivered on cost-of-living promises. (Reuters, Pew Research)

## Local and California

**5. Chino Hills sales tax measure heads to November ballot.** The city council placed Measure J, a 1-cent local sales tax, on the Nov. 3 ballot. It would raise an estimated $11.5 million a year for the general fund, exempting groceries, rent, utilities, prescriptions, and education, with required annual audits and public spending reports. (Champion, unrated local)
