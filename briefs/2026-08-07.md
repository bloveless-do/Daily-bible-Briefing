## International

**1. Iran-Oman draft deal on the Strait of Hormuz favors Tehran.** Negotiators finalized a draft agreement reopening the strait to commercial shipping, now awaiting approval from Iran's Supreme Leader. The deal creates new inbound and outbound corridors that run through Iranian waters, a departure from the decades-old system where traffic moved through Omani waters. No tolls under the temporary arrangement. Trump has said a deal "could happen" for days running, and the timing keeps slipping. (Bloomberg)

**3. DRC Ebola outbreak now the largest in the country's history.** WHO reports 3,973 confirmed cases and 1,801 deaths as of August 5, spread across five provinces and 49 health zones. ## National

**4. July payrolls fell 23,000, missing forecasts badly.** Economists expected gains in the 83,000 to 95,000 range. The unemployment rate ticked down to 4.1% from 4.2%, but that came from falling labor force participation (61.4%, the lowest in over five years), not from hiring strength. May and June were revised down a combined 103,000 jobs. (BLS)

**5. Fauci says he expects Republicans to use his testimony to prosecute him despite a Biden pardon covering conduct from 2014 through January 2025. Committee Republicans plan to skip a full Senate vote and refer the matter straight to the Justice Department. (NPR)

## Local and California

**6. Chino Hills sales tax measure headed to the November ballot.** The city council voted unanimously on July 14 to place a one-cent Transactions and Use Tax before voters November 3. It's projected to raise $11.5 million a year against a $4.3 million structural shortfall projected for FY 2026-27. Groceries, rent, utilities, prescriptions, and education are exempt. City Manager Rod Hill is touring community groups to make the case. (Champion, unrated local)

**7. Lithium-ion battery fire at a Chino warehouse.** Boxes of lithium-ion batteries caught fire early this week at a shipping warehouse on Ramona Avenue. No one was inside and no injuries were reported. Cause remains under investigation. (Champion, unrated local)
