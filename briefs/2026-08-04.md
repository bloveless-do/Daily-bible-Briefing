# Daily Brief — Tuesday, August 4, 2026

Trump calls renewed Iran talks the regime's "last chance" while US strikes stay paused and Tehran denies talks are happening. Wildfires near Spokane have destroyed more than 700 structures and forced 65,000 evacuations. Chino Hills' one-cent sales tax measure is headed to the November ballot.

## International

**1. Trump calls Iran talks the regime's "last chance," strikes stay paused.** Trump called off a planned strike campaign he said would be "the biggest attack since World War II" after Qatar, Saudi Arabia, and the UAE asked him to give diplomacy one more shot. He describes a two-phase deal: reopening the Strait of Hormuz first, denuclearization second. Iran denies any negotiations are underway, calling Trump's comments "psychological warfare," while US forces stay positioned to strike. (Bloomberg)

**2. Ukrainian drone strike kills five in Moscow region.** A drone attack on an industrial zone in the Chekhov district south of Moscow killed at least five people and injured ten, the regional governor said. It's among the deadliest strikes on the Moscow region since Russia's 2022 invasion of Ukraine. (Bloomberg)

**3. Indonesian ferry fire: 5 dead, dozens still missing.** The Mutiara Sentosa 2 caught fire off Java on Sunday with 271 people aboard. A survivor said the blaze started with a truck on the lower vehicle deck. Rescuers pulled 231 people from the water; the final count of missing is still unclear. (AP)

## National

**4. Senate Judiciary set to vote on Blanche for attorney general.** Acting AG Todd Blanche rescinded the DOJ's $1.8 billion "anti-weaponization fund," clearing the objection that had Republicans Cornyn and Tillis withholding support. Both now back the nomination, putting Blanche on track for confirmation before the Senate's five-week recess. (NPR)

**5. Spokane-area wildfires destroy 700+ structures, 65,000 evacuated.** Three fires burning together as the Spokane Complex have charred over 8,000 acres and remain at 0% containment. The Washington National Guard has been activated. State officials call it one of the worst starts to a fire season in years. (NPR)

## Local and California

**7. Chino Hills sales tax measure heads to November ballot.** The city council voted unanimously to place a one-cent sales tax on the Nov. 3 ballot, projected to bring in $11.5 million a year against a $4.3 million budget deficit. Groceries, rent, utilities, education, and prescriptions would be exempt. (Champion, unrated local)

**8. Chino Hills kidnapping-murder: suspects identified, one dead, one held without bail.** Deputies responding to gunfire on Monteverde Drive found 60-year-old Shukur Aikebaer shot in a car trunk; he and one suspect, 67-year-old Zhengfeng Bo, died at the scene. The second suspect, 66-year-old Jianquan Bo, is jailed without bail. Investigators say the men had a prior working relationship with the victim. (ABC7)

## Steelman — the other read on the top story

The strongest case against how the Iran situation has been handled: canceling a strike campaign on the strength of a deal Iran itself denies exists, then threatening "decapitation" days later while forces stay postured to hit, reads less like leverage and more like incoherence. Critics argue the ambiguity Washington treats as toughness, Tehran treats as proof the US can't be trusted to hold to any agreement it signs, which cuts against the odds of landing an actual deal and leaves the Gulf states who lobbied for restraint exposed if strikes resume anyway. (Washington Post, NPR reporting)
