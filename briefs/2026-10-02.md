UK says "strong indications" Iran played a part in a suspected plot near RAF Fairford.** Five men were arrested Sunday after three vans of masked men were seen near the base, which the US has used for strikes on Iran. ## National

**4. September jobs report misses badly.** Employers added 29,000 jobs against a median forecast of 90,000. Unemployment rose to 4.2%, partly because the workforce grew. The dollar fell and traders trimmed bets on a Fed rate hike this year. (Bloomberg, NPR)

**5. Tennessee halts executions after Christa Pike survives lethal injection.** Pike, 50, received two doses of pentobarbital Wednesday and kept breathing. Officials stopped. Gov. Bill Lee paused the remaining scheduled execution for the year and ordered a third-party review. Opponents call it the worst botched execution of the modern era, and nine Republican state lawmakers had already asked for a moratorium. Supporters of capital punishment generally frame this as a protocol failure to fix, not a verdict on the penalty. Faithful Christians split on the underlying question: Gen 9:6 and Rom 13:4 are cited for the state's authority to take life, Mic 6:8 and the Gospels' emphasis on mercy for the opposing case. (NPR)
