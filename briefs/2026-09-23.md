## National

**3. CNN, MS NOW and Politico's White House ban heads to a federal judge.** The three outlets sued after Trump said Friday he was banning them from the White House over coverage he called "fiction and lies"; a CNN reporter's press pass was pulled Saturday. Judge Timothy Kelly ordered the Justice Department to respond to the outlets' request for a temporary restraining order, with arguments expected this week. Courts have rejected similar White House access bans in the past on First Amendment grounds. (NPR, CNN, CNBC)

## Steelman — the other read on the top story

The strongest case against the "annihilate" line: it was delivered to the UN itself, the body Trump was addressing, which critics read as contempt for the institution and the diplomatic process it exists to run. On this view, threatening annihilation while simultaneously predicting a post-election deal isn't leverage, it's incoherence that tells Iran the US timeline is politics-driven rather than principle-driven, and it puts US allies who back a negotiated end to the war in an awkward position days before their own Assembly speeches.
