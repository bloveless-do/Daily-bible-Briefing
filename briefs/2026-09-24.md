# Daily Brief — Thursday, September 24, 2026

Trump threatened Iran with annihilation from the UN podium; Iran's president defied him the next day. Ethiopia's Tigray war reignited, shattering a 2022 peace deal. A federal judge ordered CNN, MS NOW, and Politico reinstated to the White House. Chino Hills holds a suicide prevention forum tonight.

## International

**1. Trump threatens Iran with "annihilation," Pezeshkian defies him at the UN.** Trump told the UN General Assembly Tuesday he could "annihilate" Iran if its leaders don't reach a deal, seven months into the US-Israel-Iran war. Iranian President Masoud Pezeshkian answered Wednesday, calling Iranians victims of US and Israeli "terrorism" and saying Iran would "fight until our last breath," while maintaining Iran seeks only peaceful nuclear technology, not weapons. Separately, Trump said envoys Steve Witkoff and Jared Kushner met roughly three hours with Iranian officials on the sidelines. (NPR)

> *"Why do the nations rage and the peoples plot in vain?" (Ps 2:1). The psalm doesn't answer with a timeline. It ends with the One enthroned unshaken and a blessing on those who take refuge in him. Worth reading whole this morning, not just the opening line.*

**2. Tigray war resumes in Ethiopia.** Tigrayan forces launched an offensive Wednesday, seizing airports at Mekelle, Shire, and reportedly Axum, and attacking federal positions in neighboring Afar and Amhara. It shatters the 2022 peace deal that ended a war estimated to have killed 600,000 people. Tigray's leaders call it defensive; an adviser to PM Abiy Ahmed calls it an all-out offensive by Tigray. Analysts warn the fighting could overlap with Sudan's war and further destabilize the Red Sea region. (NPR)

> *Jeremiah 6:14: they healed the wound of my people lightly, saying "peace, peace," when there was no peace. A signed deal is not the same thing as a settled wound. That's a description, not a verdict on who broke this one.*

**3. Trump invites Putin to G20 Miami, December.** Secretary of State Rubio confirmed Wednesday that Trump has invited Putin to the G20 summit at Trump National Doral, December 14 to 15. It would be Putin's first visit to the continental US since 2015. (The Hill)

## National

**4. Judge orders Trump administration to reinstate press access for CNN, MS NOW, Politico.** US District Judge Timothy Kelly, a Trump appointee, issued an emergency order early Thursday requiring immediate reinstatement of White House access for the three outlets, which Trump banned by social media post last Friday over their reporting. DOJ argued the reporting endangered national security; the outlets' lawyers noted that justification surfaced only after the lawsuit was filed. Kelly said due process "wasn't followed here" and set a two-week schedule for further arguments. (NPR, The Hill)

**5. Iowa judge dismisses Trump's lawsuit against pollster Ann Selzer.** Judge Scott Beattie dismissed, with prejudice, Trump's suit against Selzer, her firm, the Des Moines Register, and Gannett over a 2024 pre-election poll that showed Kamala Harris leading in Iowa, a state Trump won. The suit alleged a violation of Iowa consumer-fraud law. Beattie said allowing such claims to proceed could have a "chilling effect" on reporting and public debate. Co-plaintiffs Rep. Mariannette Miller-Meeks and former state Sen. Brad Zaun were also dismissed. (The Hill)

## Steelman — the other read on the top story

The strongest case on Iran from the left: Pezeshkian is a relative moderate within Iran's political system, and his defiance at the UN reads less as bluster than as a wartime head of state directly confronting, on the world stage, the leader of the nation he's at war with. That framing treats Trump's "annihilate" line as the escalatory move, casting Iran's response as reactive rather than aggressive, and argues the rhetoric raises the odds of miscalculation rather than lowering them. (Washington Post, news reporting)
