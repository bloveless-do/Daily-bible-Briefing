# Daily Brief — Monday, August 17, 2026

US-Iran ceasefire expired today; Trump threatens to bomb Oman over the Strait of Hormuz. Indonesia earthquake death toll hits 53. Meta's child-safety trial opened in Oakland. Chino Hills sales tax ballot argument deadline is today.

## International

**1. US-Iran ceasefire expires, Trump threatens to bomb Oman.** The 60-day US-Iran ceasefire lapsed Monday with no deal in sight. Trump said he will "bomb" Oman if it "gets in the way" of US objectives in the Strait of Hormuz, where Oman has been negotiating with Iran to reopen commercial shipping. Iran's blockade and the US naval response have cut Hormuz traffic to a bare minimum for months; roughly 20% of world oil supply normally moves through the strait. Sen. Adam Schiff said the administration's claim to a fresh 60-day window to act without Congress "has no foundation in law." (Forbes, NPR)

**2. Indonesia earthquake death toll hits 53.** Rescue crews reached villages cut off by landslides after the magnitude-7.7 quake struck off Flores island. More than 900 homes were destroyed, 5,000 people are in temporary shelters, and 235 aftershocks have followed. Over 3,500 military and police personnel are deployed. (NPR, Bloomberg)

> *"God is our refuge and strength, a very present help in trouble" (Ps 46:1). The psalm is written for exactly this kind of morning, not for predicting the next one.*

**3. NATO jet shoots down suspected Russian drone over Romania.** A Spanish F-18 on NATO air-policing duty shot down a drone that crossed into Romanian airspace from Moldova early Sunday, the fourth such shootdown over Romania this year. No injuries reported; the wreckage fell in an unpopulated area near the Ukrainian border region. (The Hill)

## National

**4. Meta's child-safety trial opens in Oakland.** Four states are trying Meta in federal court over claims that Instagram and Facebook were engineered to hook kids and that Meta concealed the mental health risks, part of a larger 29-state multidistrict case. States are seeking damages and operational changes; a Los Angeles jury already awarded $6 million in a related state case this year. (NPR)

> *"Learn to do good; seek justice, correct oppression; bring justice to the fatherless" (Isa 1:17). The prophets aim that line at nations and institutions, not just individuals. A trial over how products were built for children sits squarely in that lane.*

**5. Kushner meets Netanyahu as USS Washington heads to relieve USS Lincoln.** Jared Kushner is in Israel pressing Netanyahu to advance the postwar Gaza plan, including training a new Gaza police force and admitting an international stabilization force, after meeting Hamas leadership in Egypt. Israel is ten weeks from elections and Netanyahu is wary of concessions. Separately, the USS Washington is en route to the Middle East to relieve the USS Lincoln amid reported morale concerns aboard. (NPR)

**6. Trump orders cut to US-South Korea military drills.** Trump directed Defense Secretary Hegseth to "substantially reduce" the Ulchi Freedom Shield exercises that began Monday, citing his relationship with Kim Jong Un and noting South Korea declined to help pressure Iran. The drills proceeded as scheduled while the scope of any reduction remains unclear. (Axios)

## Local and California
