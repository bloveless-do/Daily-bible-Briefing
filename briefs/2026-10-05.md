## National

**4. US-Russia Ukraine talks include a Lukoil asset deal.** The New York Times reported that the talks include a multibillion-dollar deal for Lukoil to acquire oil fields, refineries and gas stations abroad, floated by Putin at a Sept. 5 meeting with Witkoff and Kushner. The backers include two Middle Eastern groups that have done business with Witkoff and Kushner, plus investor and Trump donor Todd Boehly. It is not established that Witkoff or Kushner would profit. A senior administration official said they are negotiating terms to secure "a substantial upfront payment and profits for the United States." (The Hill, citing NYT)

**5. Supreme Court opens its term with Suncor v. Boulder.** Boulder, Colorado is suing Suncor and Exxon Mobil, alleging the companies misled the public about fossil fuel harms and caused costly disasters. (NPR, The Hill)

**6. Suspected pneumonic plague case in Russia under US and Russian monitoring.** A lab worker in her late 20s died of pneumonia of unknown origin at an Irkutsk institute. The cause is unconfirmed. (The Hill)
