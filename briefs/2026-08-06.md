# Daily Brief — Thursday, August 6, 2026

Russia hit Kyiv with its heaviest barrage in weeks, killing 17. Progressive Abdul El-Sayed won Michigan's Senate primary and Trump called him a "man of hate" the next day. A gas explosion hurt nine people, six of them children, in Chino Hills.

## International

**1. Russia hits Kyiv with 24 missiles and 115 drones, kills 17.** Russia fired 24 ballistic missiles, several hypersonic or supersonic cruise missiles, and 115 drones at Kyiv overnight in a barrage that lasted about two hours. Ukraine's air defenses shot down only one ballistic missile. Zelenskyy said that is because Ukraine has run short of interceptors for its Patriot batteries, the only system that can stop ballistic missiles, not because defenders failed to try. (NPR)

> *Ps 46:1-3, 10. "God is our refuge and strength... though the nations rage, though the kingdoms totter." The psalm does not say when the shelling stops. It says who is not moved by it.*

**2. Drone carrying explosive device found near German airport runway.** An airport employee spotted a drone near the south runway at Leipzig/Halle Airport carrying what officials believe is an explosive device. No detonation occurred and no injuries were reported. A German security official called it a "new quality" of threat, since past drone incidents at German airports have mostly involved surveillance rather than munitions. (NPR)

**3. Bangladesh's ousted leader says she is coming home despite a death sentence.** Sheikh Hasina, sentenced to death in absentia last November for ordering deadly force against 2024 protesters, said on the second anniversary of her ouster that she plans to return from exile in India. New Delhi has not responded to Dhaka's extradition request, and Hasina disputes the verdict. (NPR)

> *Dan 2:21. God "removes kings and sets up kings." That is a claim about who is ultimately in charge, not a verdict on Hasina's guilt or Bangladesh's politics.*

## National

**4. Progressive Abdul El-Sayed wins Michigan's Democratic Senate primary; Trump calls him a "man of hate."** El-Sayed, an epidemiologist running on Medicare for All and an end to aid for Israel's Gaza campaign, beat moderate Rep. Haley Stevens by roughly 1 point despite being outspent about 9 to 1, including by AIPAC's political arm. Trump first called the result "great news for the Republican Party," then a day later called El-Sayed a "man of hate" over his record on Israel. El-Sayed faces former Rep. Mike Rogers (R) in November in a race that could decide Senate control. (NPR, The Hill)

**5. Senate panel votes today on holding Fauci in contempt of Congress.** The Senate Homeland Security Committee, chaired by Rand Paul, votes Thursday on whether to hold Anthony Fauci in contempt after he invoked the Fifth Amendment 111 times last week rather than answer questions about the COVID-19 response. Fauci says Paul has "an unhinged obsession" with him. A 2025 Biden pardon covers his conduct through January 2025 but would not shield him from anything tied to this year's testimony. A yes vote sends the matter to DOJ to decide on prosecution. (NPR, The Hill)

**6. Dr. Erica Schwartz confirmed as CDC director.** The Senate confirmed Schwartz to lead the CDC, filling a post that had been vacant. (NPR)

## Local and California

**7. Gas leak explosion injures nine, six of them children, in Chino Hills.** A house on Sierra Vista Drive exploded Sunday afternoon from a gas leak of unknown origin, hurling the garage door into a parked car and blowing out windows in nearby homes. Chino Valley Fire evacuated 16 homes; five sustained damage. Four of the injured went to Arrowhead Regional Medical Center by ambulance. Displaced families returned home around midnight. (Champion, unrated local)

> *Ps 34:18. "The Lord is near to the brokenhearted." Worth saying plainly for the families still cleaning up broken windows this week.*

**8. Chino Hills deputies fatally shoot kidnapping suspect after victim killed.** A man and an accomplice kidnapped a victim and placed him in the trunk of a car; the victim was killed. San Bernardino County deputies shot and killed the suspect on Wandering Ridge Drive. (Champion, unrated local)

## Steelman — the other read on the top story

The strongest case for El-Sayed's win: he overcame a nine-to-one spending disadvantage against an AIPAC-backed opponent by running an unapologetically progressive campaign on healthcare costs and opposition to the Gaza war, built on real DSA and UAW-aligned organizing rather than outsider name recognition alone. Calling that result the work of a "hater," as Trump did, forecloses the actual policy argument, which is about US support for Israel's conduct in Gaza, not personal animus. Centrist Democrats who call the closer-than-expected margin a warning sign have a point too, but the win itself was earned against the money, not despite a lack of real support. (Axios reporting)
