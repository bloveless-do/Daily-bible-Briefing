# Daily Brief — Monday, August 3, 2026

Trump says Iran talks resume today; Tehran denies any are scheduled. Wildfires have forced 60,000 out of Spokane. A Chino Hills kidnapping ended in a fatal deputy shooting, suspects identified this week.

## International

**1. Trump says Iran talks resume today, Iran says no such talks are planned.** Trump announced over the weekend he called off a new round of planned strikes on Iran, saying talks would begin Monday covering the Strait of Hormuz and Iran's nuclear program, and calling a deal "imminent." Iran's foreign ministry spokesman told reporters Monday there are no negotiations scheduled with the US, only a separate track with Oman on reopening safe passage through Hormuz, reportedly in its final stages. Oil fell about 4.5% on the news. (Bloomberg)

## National

**3. GOP holdouts still blocking Blanche's confirmation as attorney general.** Sens. John Cornyn and Thom Tillis continue withholding votes on Todd Blanche's nomination, citing a proposed $1.8 billion "anti-weaponization" fund for Trump allies and a deal shielding Trump and his businesses from future tax audits. Trump has floated waiting out Cornyn's and Tillis's terms rather than compromising, and separately threatened to revive the fund through legislation. (Axios, The Hill)

**4. Wildfires force 60,000 from Spokane, destroy 600 structures.** Fires driven by 35 to 45 mph winds burned into Riverside State Park and across the Spokane River into the city over the weekend. No injuries or missing persons reported as of Sunday, though officials called it early in the response. Evacuees included patients from the VA hospital. Roughly 386 square miles have burned statewide, none of the major fires contained, with no rain in the forecast. (NPR)

## Local and California

**5. Chino Hills kidnap-murder suspects identified, one dead.** Shukur Aikebaer, 60, was shot and abducted from his Monteverde Drive home Wednesday night after his power went out, in what investigators tie to a prior business dispute over a $20 million Pasadena apartment project. Deputies stopped the suspects' vehicle; passenger Jianquan Bo, 66, was arrested on suspicion of murder and kidnapping, while driver Zhengfeng Bo, 67, was shot and killed by deputies after opening fire from the trunk. Aikebaer died at the scene. (ABC7, KTLA)

**6. Two killed in early-morning Tesla crash on 60 Freeway in Chino.** A 21-year-old woman and a man in his 30s died around 2 a.m. Monday when their Tesla lost control near Reservoir Street, hit the center divider, and went up an embankment. A woman in her 20s and a baby in the car were hospitalized in stable condition. Westbound lanes reopened by 5:30 a.m. Cause under investigation. (ABC7)

## Steelman — the other read on the top story

The NYT's read on the broader Iran campaign: Washington has not converted military superiority into strategic success. Iran has proven more resilient than expected, retains real leverage over Hormuz, and its political leadership survived the strikes intact even as its nuclear and missile programs were only degraded, not eliminated. On this reading, today's "imminent deal" talk is Iran managing the clock, not the US closing out a win. (NYT news analysis)
