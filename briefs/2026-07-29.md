# Daily Brief — Wednesday, July 29, 2026

Trump and Netanyahu call their Iran-war meeting "productive" hours before Iran fired missiles at a US base in Jordan, all intercepted. Lindsey Graham's sister was sworn in to finish his Senate term. Chino Hills' sales tax measure is confirmed for the November ballot.

## International

**1. Trump-Netanyahu meeting, then Iran fires on a US base in Jordan.** Trump and Netanyahu held a private 90-minute Oval Office meeting Tuesday, their first since the Iran war began, and both sides called it "productive." They discussed the war, a Lebanon peace framework, and possible Saudi-Israel normalization. Hours after the meeting ended, Iran's IRGC fired ballistic missiles at a US base and a Central Command site in Jordan. CENTCOM said all were intercepted and called it an attempted surprise attack. (Axios, CENTCOM statement)

**2. Wildfires still burning in France and Spain, another heat wave coming.** Fires that started in France's Gironde region over a week ago have burned roughly 104,000 acres there and 190,000 in Spain, with more than 300,000 people evacuated, France's largest peacetime evacuation since World War II. The Gironde fire is stabilized but not out. A fourth heat wave is forecast this week with no rain. (NPR, Reuters via Axios)

**3. Ukraine's negotiator says Kyiv now has leverage.** Sergiy Kyslytsya, a senior Zelenskyy aide, told NPR Ukraine's long-range drone strikes on Russian oil and gas facilities are causing fuel shortages and that Russia's army is no longer advancing meaningfully. US-mediated talks have stalled for months while Washington focused on Iran, though contact with American and occasional Russian envoys continues. (NPR)

## National

**4. Senate confirms Jay Clayton as Director of National Intelligence.** The Senate confirmed Clayton 51-47, a straight party-line vote, ending Bill Pulte's acting tenure. At his July 16 confirmation hearing Clayton said he is not an "election denier" but declined to say directly that Trump lost the 2020 election. (The Hill)

**5. Lindsey Graham's sister sworn in to finish his Senate term.** Funeral services for Sen. Lindsey Graham, who died of an aortic dissection at 71, ran Tuesday in Washington and continued Wednesday in Columbia, South Carolina. Trump spoke at the National Cathedral service. Gov. Henry McMaster, at Trump's recommendation, appointed Graham's sister, Darline Graham, to hold the seat until January 2027; a special primary is set for August 11 to determine who runs for the seat in November. (CBS News, NBC News)

## Local and California

**6. Chino Hills' one-cent sales tax measure locked in for November.** The city council placed the measure on the Nov. 3 ballot; if approved it would bring in an estimated $11.5 million a year in new revenue. (Champion, unrated local)
