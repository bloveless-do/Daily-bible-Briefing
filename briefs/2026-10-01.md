## Steelman: the other read on the top story

On the Hegseth cuts, the strongest left-of-center case: critics say the issue is less the number of generals than who is removed and why. Hegseth has blocked some colonels from promotion and fired several senior officers without public explanation. Sen. Jack Reed (D-R.I.), top Democrat on Armed Services, argued that personnel decisions should rest on analysis, not "arbitrary percentages," and that cutting experienced officers without justification "could cripple" the military rather than make it efficient. Hegseth's counter is the ratio: 17 four- and five-star officers for 12 million WWII troops against 44 for 2.1 million today. (Washington Post, NPR, Axios)
