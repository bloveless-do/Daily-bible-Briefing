# Daily Brief — Friday, September 11, 2026

25 years since 9/11, ceremonies split by politics this year. Houthis seize a Red Sea port near a key shipping strait. Newsom signs the nation's toughest AI chatbot child-safety law.

## International

**1. Houthis seize Yemen's Mokha port, raising Red Sea shipping risk.** Iran-backed Houthi forces captured the port city of Mokha on Yemen's Red Sea coast, their biggest territorial gain since a 2022 truce. Mokha sits about 46 miles from the Bab el-Mandeb Strait, the chokepoint carriers have been cautiously returning to after two years of rerouting around Africa. Oil held above $100 a barrel on the supply-risk news. (Reuters, NPR)

**2. Trump says no regrets on Iran strikes; Treasury sanctions Hezbollah network.** Trump said he'd "do exactly" what he did in the Iran war if he had to do it again. Treasury added sanctions on firms and individuals in Iraq, the UAE, Lebanon and Turkey accused of funding Hezbollah and other Iranian proxies. (Reuters, AP)

Mayor Mamdani, the city's first Muslim mayor, is attending despite a 100,000-signature petition asking him to stay away and public criticism from Rudy Giuliani. Gov. ## Local and California

**5. Newsom signs nation's toughest child-safety AI chatbot and social media laws.** The package fines large platforms up to $1 million per child found harmed, bans addictive feeds for under-16 users, and requires AI chatbot operators to run risk assessments and suicide/self-harm safeguards before launch. One bill was inspired by a teenager's death after being coached by a chatbot. Meta objects that the addictive-feed restriction limits its ability to serve users. (AP)

**6. Chino Hills: emergency-prep workshop held, Din Tai Fung rumored for a local opening.** The city ran a free emergency-preparedness workshop Thursday at the Community Center covering family plans, notifications, and evacuation procedures. Separately, the Champion reports Din Tai Fung is rumored to be eyeing a Chino Hills location, with The Shoppes, Crossroads Marketplace, and The Commons all mentioned as possible sites. (Champion, unrated local)
