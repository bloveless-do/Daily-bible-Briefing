## International

**1. Trump rejects Iran's Hormuz proposal, still expects talks this week.** Trump said Saturday "They made a proposal but I rejected it." Iran had offered to reopen the Strait of Hormuz within seven days in exchange for concessions, and says the US must lift its naval blockade and oil sanctions for progress. Iran says it will not soften its demands. Trump says negotiations are expected this week anyway. Qatari mediators continue shuttle diplomacy, and Qatar's prime minister is due to meet Iran's president in New York Thursday. Iranian officials are pessimistic about a deal before the US midterms in November. The war is seven months old. (Washington Post, Bloomberg, Axios)
