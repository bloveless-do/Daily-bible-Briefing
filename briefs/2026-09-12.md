**2. Saudi Arabia's East-West pipeline stays shut after drone strikes; Houthis press Red Sea gains.** Saudi Arabia shut the pipeline as a precaution after it was hit by drones launched from Iraq, injuring several workers. The strikes came as Iran-backed Houthi forces continued advancing along Yemen's Red Sea coast, threatening shipping through the Bab el-Mandeb Strait. Oil prices remain elevated on the supply risk. (Washington Post, NPR)

## National

**3. NTSB investigates fatal Amazon cargo plane crash in Miami.** NTSB Chair Jennifer Homendy said all five people killed when a 21 Air Boeing 767 overran the runway on September 6 were in two vehicles struck by the plane. Investigators recovered both the flight data recorder and cockpit voice recorder; a 32-person team is examining flight crew training, maintenance history, and the airport's runway overrun protections. Cause has not been determined. (AP)

**4. A year after Charlie Kirk's killing, NPR examines the doxing culture it left behind.** Kirk was assassinated at Utah Valley University last September. In the aftermath, websites compiled names, photos, and employers of people who had criticized him online, and the campaign ran for months before legal pushback and meme culture wore it down. A year later, NPR reports the damage to how neighbors treat each other online has not healed. (NPR)

## Local and California

**5. Chino eliminates $1,230 fee for appealing Planning Commission decisions.** The City Council voted to drop the fee after resident Bob Nigg was charged it to appeal the Planning Commission's ruling on the Eden project. (Champion, unrated local)

**6. Chino Hills: Pop-Up City Hall and Paws at the Park today, Peyton Drive project underway.** The city held Pop-Up City Hall at the Stater Bros. on Chino Hills Parkway this morning, letting residents talk directly with the mayor, council, police, and fire district. Paws at the Park ran the same morning at Vila Borba Dog Park. Separately, the Peyton Drive pavement rehabilitation project between State Route 71 and Grand Avenue is underway. This week's Chino Planning Commission meeting was canceled. (Champion, unrated local)
