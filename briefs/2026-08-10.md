# Daily Brief — Monday, August 10, 2026

Iran's war with the US drags past five months with no end signaled, even as a Hormuz deal with Oman nears. Trump renews his push to fire Fed Governor Lisa Cook. Wildfires force evacuations across the West. Chino Hills makes its case for a November sales tax hike.

## International

**1. Iran sets conditions for reopening the Strait of Hormuz, deal with Oman close but not sufficient on its own.** Iran's Supreme National Security Council says the strait will not return to normal traffic until the US "corrects its behavior." Tehran is demanding the US lift its naval blockade, end sanctions, release frozen Iranian assets, end the war, and pay compensation for damages, on top of a separate management deal it is close to finalizing with Oman. The war began February 28 with US and Israeli strikes on Iran. Trump told Axios the US is only "semi-negotiating," betting that Iran's inflation and inability to pay its own troops will force capitulation. (Forbes, Axios)

**2. Ukraine keeps hitting Russian refineries, Russia feeling the fuel pinch.** Ukraine has struck Russian oil facilities with long-range drones almost daily for months, and the campaign is producing fuel shortages and public frustration inside Russia. (NPR)

## National

**3. Trump renews push to remove Fed Governor Lisa Cook.** The White House sent Cook a letter August 5 giving her until August 26 to respond to 2021 mortgage fraud allegations, reviving an effort the Supreme Court temporarily blocked in a 5-4 ruling in June while a legal challenge proceeds. Cook, who has never been charged, denies wrongdoing; her attorneys call it an attempt to interfere with the Fed's independence. (Axios, NPR, Washington Post)

**4. July jobs report lands ugly.** The economy shed 23,000 jobs in July against expectations of a 91,000 gain, and the two prior months were revised down a combined 103,000. (Axios)

**5. Wildfires force evacuations across the West.** The National Interagency Fire Center logged 183 new fires since Saturday, including eight large ones, with 94 large fires burning nationwide and more than 30,000 personnel assigned. Evacuations are in place in Utah, where nearly a dozen active fires have burned over 300,000 acres. Canada is at its highest national preparedness level, with international crews arriving from Mexico, New Zealand, and Australia. (NPR, AP)

## Local and California

**6. Chino Hills city manager pitches the November sales tax measure.** City Manager Rod Hill was the guest speaker tonight at the Chino Valley Democratic Club, laying out the city budget and the case for the one-cent sales tax measure headed to the Nov. 3 ballot. (Champion, unrated local)

**7. FBI searches homes and offices tied to County Supervisor Curt Hagman.** On July 28 the FBI served search warrants at Hagman's Chino home and a Chino Hills office believed to be his, along with four other locations connected to figures on the Ontario International Airport Authority board. The warrants are sealed, no charges have been filed, and the FBI has not said what prompted the searches. (Champion, unrated local; ABC7, KTLA)
