## International

**1. US and Iran exchange strikes after a month-long lull; conflict enters its seventh month.** CENTCOM said it struck three Iranian oil tankers Saturday after Iran's Revolutionary Guard Corps fired two ballistic missiles at a US Navy aircraft carrier and destroyer patrolling near the Strait of Hormuz. Neither ship was hit and no US personnel were injured. US forces reported permanently disabling two tankers and destroying a third; two more tankers were hit in the Gulf of Oman and near Jask, with crews forced to abandon ship. Energy Secretary Chris Wright said Sunday the US may not reach a deal to stop Iran from getting a nuclear weapon. (Reuters, NPR, CNN)

**2. US envoys call Kyiv talks "substantive" after meeting Putin in Moscow, no breakthrough announced.** Jared Kushner and Steve Witkoff met Zelenskyy in Kyiv Sunday, their first visit as negotiators, a day after three hours with Putin in Moscow produced no announced deal. Witkoff said he was "very encouraged." A White House official told Reuters next steps would come in the coming weeks. British, French, and German national security advisers were in Kyiv the same day for follow-on talks. (Reuters, NPR)

**3. AfD wins historic plurality in German state election, falls short of a governing majority.** The Alternative for Germany took 44.4% of the vote in Sunday's Saxony-Anhalt election, the strongest state-level showing for a far-right party in Germany since World War Two. Chancellor Friedrich Merz's conservatives took 18.3%. Final counts left AfD three seats short of a majority in the 83-seat legislature. Every mainstream party has ruled out governing with it, and Germany's domestic intelligence service classifies the state party as right-wing extremist, so the win has no clear path to power yet. (NPR, Reuters)

## National

**4. Amazon-branded cargo plane overruns Miami runway, five dead.** A Boeing 767-300 freighter operated by 21 Air for Amazon overran the runway landing at Miami International Airport Sunday afternoon, struck multiple vehicles, and caught fire. Five people were killed and five injured, three critically. The flight originated in San Juan, Puerto Rico. NTSB Chair Jennifer Homendy is leading the investigation. (AP, NPR, CNN)

## Local and California

**6. Chino Hills' 1-cent sales tax, Measure J, is officially on the Nov. 3 ballot.** The council placed the measure on the ballot after moving toward it since summer. If approved, it raises the local sales tax by one percentage point and is projected to generate about $11.5 million a year in city-controlled revenue, with mandated public disclosure and independent annual audits. (Champion, unrated local)

**7. Chino City Council took up its 20-year General Plan on Sept. 2.** The plan, which sets Chino's development blueprint through 2046, was approved by the Planning Commission on Aug. 20 and went to council Sept. 2 for a public hearing. It rezones select properties for multi-family housing and creates four new land-use designations: downtown, boulevard mixed use, regional mixed use, and employment mixed use. (Champion, unrated local)

## Steelman — the other read on the top story

The strongest case against how the Iran war has been run: seven months in, the administration has never declared an end state, cycling from demands for Iran's "unconditional surrender" to signaling openness to a deal that just swaps one hardline ayatollah for another.
