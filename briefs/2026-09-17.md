# Daily Brief - Thursday, September 17, 2026

EU offers Canada associate membership, Trump threatens tariffs over it. Fed hikes rates to 3.75%-4%, first increase since 2023. Chino Valley's November ballot lineup is set.

## International

**1. EU proposes Canada as its first "associate member," Trump threatens tariffs in response.** European Commission President Ursula von der Leyen used her State of the Union address in Strasbourg to propose making Canada the EU's first associate member, covering manufacturing cooperation, a tech alliance, joint defense industrial work, and an Arctic project. The offer follows Trump's tariffs on Canadian goods and his repeated talk of Canada as the "51st state." Trump called the plan "laughable" and said he would put "very serious tariffs" on the EU if he judges it a "hostile act." (Reuters, NPR, CNBC)

**2. Trump says the Iran war may be nearing an end.** Trump told reporters the US could be close to ending the roughly seven-month conflict with Iran, saying Tehran "wants to make a deal" and that he has spoken with Iran "directly." He gave no terms. The war began in February, has expanded into Yemen, and continues to disrupt oil markets. (CNBC)

**3. Duterte appears in person at the ICC for the first time.** Former Philippine President Rodrigo Duterte appeared in the ICC courtroom in The Hague for the first time since his March 2025 transfer, at a hearing on his fitness to stand trial for murder charges tied to his drug-war killings. Trial is set to begin in November. His lawyers argue cognitive decline makes him unfit; he did not speak in court. (Washington Post, Bloomberg)

## National

**4. Fed raises rates to 3.75%-4%, first hike since 2023.** The Fed's rate-setting committee voted 12-0 to raise its benchmark rate a quarter point, citing inflation kept elevated by rising oil prices. Updated projections point to one more hike before year end. (CNBC)

**5. House holds billionaire Leon Black in contempt over Epstein probe.** The House Oversight Committee, then the full House, voted to hold investor Leon Black in contempt for defying subpoenas about his business relationship with Jeffrey Epstein. Black sued the committee to block the subpoenas, calling it a "fishing expedition that oversteps its authority." The matter goes to the Justice Department, which will decide whether to pursue charges. (Washington Post, CNN)

## Local and California

**6. Chino Valley's November 3 candidate lineup is set.** The nomination period has closed. In Chino, Councilman Curtis Burton faces Jimmy Alexandris for the District 2 seat. Council races: Ray Marquez unopposed in District 1, Shanita Ayala vs. Peter Rogers in District 2, Brian Johsz vs. Josue Estrada in District 4. (Champion, unrated local; City of Chino Hills candidate filing)

## Steelman - the other read on the top story

The strongest case against Trump's tariff threat here: critics argue the "hostile act" he's reacting to is one his own trade war provoked. Canada didn't pursue an EU associate-membership track until after 50% US tariffs and "51st state" talk made Washington look unreliable as a partner. On this read, the EU offer isn't European aggression, it's the direct and foreseeable cost of treating an ally like an adversary, and threatening tariffs to stop it only deepens the rift. (Washington Post, "Battle with Trump is sending Canada into the arms of the E.U.")
