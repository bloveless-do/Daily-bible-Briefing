Officials list about 195 people missing; civilian-run databases put the number closer to 4,000. Roughly 1,600 buildings are damaged or collapsed, including the pediatric and neonatal wards of a hospital in Cali. ## National

**4. Medicaid ends funding for gender-transition care in minors.** HHS finalized a rule Tuesday barring Medicaid and CHIP, which together cover 35.5 million children, from funding puberty blockers, hormone therapy, and surgeries for gender dysphoria in minors, effective October. Coverage phases out by April 2027 for minors already on hormone treatment; mental health coverage continues. Trump directed CMS administrator Dr. Mehmet Oz to make the change, saying the government will not pay for "barbaric surgeries." Major medical groups, including the American Academy of Pediatrics, oppose the restriction and say treatment decisions belong to patients, families, and doctors. Legal advocates plan to challenge the rule in court. (AP)

Faithful Christians land on both sides of this one. The administration's case: minors cannot legally consent to other major irreversible medical decisions, and the state should not subsidize ones that carry lasting physical consequences. The opposing case: established clinical guidelines support these treatments for some adolescents, and the government is substituting its judgment for that of parents and treating physicians. Both arguments deserve a fair hearing rather than a verse used as a tiebreaker.

**5. Treasury permanently ends beneficial ownership reporting for US companies.** FinCEN finalized a rule Tuesday ending the requirement that domestic companies and individuals report beneficial ownership information under the 2021 Corporate Transparency Act, a law aimed at combating shell-company money laundering. Foreign companies registered in the US still must report on their foreign owners. FinCEN says it will delete previously filed data belonging to now-exempt US persons, an estimated $9 billion a year in compliance costs removed. (US Treasury Department; also Reuters)
