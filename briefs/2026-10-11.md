# Daily Brief — Sunday, October 11, 2026

Houthi missile strike on Riyadh airport kills 12; Trump weighs joining Saudi strikes. US-Russia diesel deal eases sanctions, Trump tells Ukraine to "get a new leader." Pentagon plans livestreamed firing squad execution of Nidal Hasan.

## International

**1. Missile attack on Riyadh airport kills 12, wounds 300+.** Saudi authorities blame the Houthis for the Saturday attack on King Khalid International Airport, the deadliest on any Gulf Arab state since the Iran war began. It followed a week of Houthi strikes on Saudi airports, military sites and oil infrastructure, launched after a Saudi-led offensive against the Houthis. The US has supplied intelligence, target identification and planning support. Trump said he is weighing joining the strikes ("We'll find out") and plans to speak with Crown Prince Mohammed bin Salman. Airport operations are suspended through at least 9 p.m. Sunday. (NPR, AP via The Hill)

**2. US and Russia announce diesel deal easing some sanctions.** The two governments say the deal will reopen fuel flows. Zelenskyy criticized it. Trump responded that Ukraine should "get a new leader." Kyiv pushed back. Separately, a Russian strike in Zaporizhzhia killed 23, and Ukrainian drones hit a data center near Moscow. (NPR, AP via The Hill)

**3. Pentagon raises US death toll in Iran operations to 21.** (The Hill)

## National

**4. Pentagon plans public, possibly livestreamed firing squad execution of Nidal Hasan.** The Dec. 3 execution at Fort Hood would be the first US public execution since the 1930s. Hasan was convicted in the 2009 Fort Hood shooting that killed 13. Pope Leo XIV called capital punishment "inadmissible" on World Day Against the Death Penalty. Cardinal Parolin and the US bishops' conference president urged reconsideration. Criticism is bipartisan: Sens. Tillis, Murkowski and Curtis object, Sen. Kelly wants Hegseth removed, and Vice President Vance says he would not watch. Trump says he will make the final call on the livestream. (NPR, The Hill)
